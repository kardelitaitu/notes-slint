## Question

The thing being decided, in one or two sentences. Write it so a reader six months from now
knows what was actually in dispute — not what the answer happened to be.

## Options

### A. Name of option

What it is. What it costs. What breaks.

### B. Name of option

Same.

## Recommendation

**A**, because …

Be decisive. A note that concludes "both have merits" has not finished the work.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, and what becomes harder or impossible. Name anything this forecloses —
those are the parts people remember wrongly later.

<!-- required for rejected notes; useful elsewhere -->
