`crates/xtask/src/smoke.rs` by line, and that file has moved under them: `:4203-4211` is now the

## Two lists both called "STRIP-4 owes"

This is the discrepancy the "4 of 6" prose was counting against, and it is why that number felt right
and is still wrong. `crates/bridge-slint/src/product.rs:44-52` names **four** debts on the module
header; ADR-0006's bullet 6 cites `:52`, which is **the fourth one only**.

- The three the reckoning at `0832d08d` just paid - **the geometry settle episode, the honest
  shutdown, the panic hook** - are items **(1), (2) and (3)** of the product.rs block, and their
  proofs
  are named in `.agents/notes/implemented/2026-09-16-strip4-debt-reckoning.md`: `2fc924f3` for the
  settle episode, `PRODUCT_CLOSE_NEEDLES` (`smoke.rs:4075`) for the shutdown, and
  `crates/bridge-slint/tests/panic_hook.rs` (`ebbb755c`) for the hook.
- **ADR bullet 6 is item (4)**: the exists-mark, the legend keys, the pin check mark. Bullet 6 was
  never about settle/shutdown/hook, and paying (1)-(3) does not close it.

Both lists are real. Name them apart, or the next count of the gate will be wrong in the same
direction.

## Recommendation

**Report the gate as one green, three partial, two blocked by this machine - and do not delete
`bridge-gpui` on any reading of today.**

> **ONE of six green, three partial, two blocked by a machine that currently drops injected input, and
> the day's earlier 4-of-6 prose count was the manager grading a different list than the ADR wrote.**

Three honest riders on that sentence. (a) Row 3 being green moves **nothing** in §9: `git remote -v`
is empty, so no CI has ever run, and the honesty gate is symmetric (AGENTS.md). (b) Rows 4 and 5
are not
"unwritten" - the legs and the needles exist; what this box cannot supply is a press that lands, which
is a machine fact and not a code gap, and it is recorded here as machine-blocked rather than quietly
demoted to owed. (c) A gate that can only be graded upward is a narrative, so the "4 of 6" is
retracted in favour of the table above, and the table is the thing to cite.

## Reopening conditions

Per row - the specific fact that turns it green, and what it does *not* take.

- **Row 1 (recents trio) - a render leg.** Unlock: a product leg that opens the real popup, reads a
  recents-shaped line off a live window's own stderr, and matches the row labels to it. A fourth unit
  test over `recents_rows` does not unlock it. The coupling is real: opening that popup by pointer
  needs the row-4/5 unlock, or a door to the `TouchArea` that does not go through the OS pointer.
- **Row 2 (§4.5 on TextEdit) - the live widget.** Unlock: a byte-identical round trip driven through a
  real Slint `TextEdit` - the pump's identical-check on real widget bytes - not through a copied `lf`
  in a headless twin. Deleting the copy is what closes this, not asserting over it harder.
- **Row 3 (rect fixed point) - reopen on decline.** It is green; reopen it the day a run reports
  `ProductCycle::NotJudged`, or a drift line other than `(0,0,0,0)` on a station that can host the
  cycle, or a claim of CI's copy of the row while CI does not exist.
- **Row 4 (dialogs) - two keys, two locks.** The **input-desktop fix unlocks the Open half only**:
  press lands, ask reaches Rust, dialog appears. **Save As is owed on any box** - a leg must press
  grid row 1, and then assert the loaded or written **text appears in the window**, which is the
  assertion
  `smoke.rs:6452` is still disclaiming.
- **Row 5 (autosave toggle by menu) - the same desktop fix, no substitute.** Unlock: a live run whose
  `menu-click` leg reports `menu: autosave-row toggled` on real stderr and gets a judged verdict
  instead of NOT JUDGED. The Ctrl+T chord leg passing is explicitly not the condition.
- **Row 6 (STRIP-4 bullet) - the pin half only, and it is now the LAST arrow.** The wiring is paid
  as of `bfc9d50f` (the answer flips the real model; the census proves the cell reads that very bit
  and that
  the click writes nothing). Unlock the bullet: a press that reaches the pin cell on a live window and
  the glyph swapping to `icons/pin.svg` in amber with the bar's ground following - the paint leg the
  test's own header names as NOT PROVEN. The exists-mark and the legend are already paid; do not let a
  future reader reopen them, and do not let the paid two carry the unpaid one.

And the law the whole table stands under, unchanged: **until all six are green the §9 count moves
neither way** - not up on this bridge's account, for want of CI, and not down on gpui's, frozen with
its proofs still compiling.
