# archived

Notes that are **no longer operative**: superseded, obsolete, or graduated into an ADR.

- Frontmatter `status: archived`.
- Frozen. Do not edit the body of an archived note — if it needs correcting, unarchive it
  to `../proposed/` and say why at the top.
- An archived note should usually have `decision:` set, pointing at the ADR that replaced it
  (`docs/decisions/NNNN-....md`).
- For archaeology only. Nothing here should be cited as a current reason to do or not do
  something — check `../proposed/`, `../implemented/`, and `../rejected/` for that.
- Keep the reason for archiving in the note body: `> Superseded by ADR-NNNN on YYYY-MM-DD`.
